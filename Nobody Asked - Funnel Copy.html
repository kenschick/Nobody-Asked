Why the split matters

Mixing these stages inside one asset is the most common way a platform like this goes sideways: an execution that opens with TOFU posture and closes with a BOFU warranty claim is doing two jobs and will do neither well. Tagging each line's stage before it reaches a brief keeps the funnel discipline intact as the platform scales across formats.
